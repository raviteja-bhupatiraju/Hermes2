- Work: *Hermes med petasos, drejet mod h. skulder* (Hermes with petasos, turned towards the right shoulder), plaster

- Photograph: `hermes-bust-smk-kas1636.jpg`, from Wikimedia Commons,
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ubekendt,_Hermes_med_petasos,_drejet_mod_h_skulder,_,_KAS1636,_Statens_Museum_for_Kunst.jpg>
- License: public domain (no attribution required; credited here and in the About dialog anyway).
- `hermes-bust.png` is the bust cut out of the photograph by `segment_bust.py`; `gen_icons.py` tones it in the colors
  of the brand and draws all icons, the splash screen and the installer images from it.
