Searching Documents

Search the field values of all documents of your projects, of every MedBiquitous standard, for a word, a phrase or a regular expression, optionally limited to certain kinds of fields, and open the matching fields in the editor.

Search

  1. Choose Search > Search Documents… or press Ctrl+H. The Search dialog opens on the Hermes Document Search page.
  2. In Containing text, type what to find. Selected text is filled in, and earlier searches are available in the drop-down list.
  3. Choose options as needed:
    • Case sensitive: match upper and lower case exactly.
    • Whole word: match only whole words.
    • Regular expression: interpret the text as a Java regular expression, for example CM[EN] or D0\d{5}. An invalid expression is reported in the dialog.
  4. Under Search In, keep All fields or select field groups (see below). The groups are listed under the name of their standard. At least one group must be chosen.
  5. Choose the Scope: Workspace (all projects), Selected resources, Enclosing projects (the projects of the selection) or a Working set.
  6. Click Search.

Field groups

Every standard defines its own field groups, shown in the dialog under the standard's name (a standard with several versions, such as Curriculum Inventory, is listed once). Selecting groups limits the search to their fields; All fields searches every field of every document.

Other standards

The other standards have groups for their main parts, for example Titles, Events, Expectations (competencies), Academic levels and Sequence blocks of a Curriculum Inventory. The groups of each standard are listed in its topic under The MedBiquitous Standards.

What is searched

  • Element text and attribute values, so you can search for MeSH descriptor IDs (D018805) and other codes as well as for text.
  • All documents of the projects in the scope: Healthcare LOM records (XML and JSON) and documents of the other standards. Documents outside projects are not searched; to search any folder, use hermes search on the command line (see Validate outside Hermes 2).
  • Documents with unsaved changes are searched as edited, not as saved.

Work with the results

The Search view lists the matches grouped by project, document and field, with the number of matches and the matching text highlighted. The view's title summarizes the search: the query, the scope, the number of matches and the number of documents.

  • Double-click a field to open its document and select the field in the editor; double-click a document to open it.
  • Hover over a field to see its element path and full value.
  • Use the Search view's tool bar to step through matches, switch between the tree and a flat list, run the search again or go back to earlier searches.
