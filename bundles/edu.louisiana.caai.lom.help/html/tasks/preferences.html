Setting Preferences

Choose Window > Preferences… to set the defaults for new documents, your author identity, validation, the MeSH online fallback and keyboard shortcuts.

Healthcare LOM > Terminologies

Lists the included terminologies with their version, source and license notice. There is nothing to set; see Offline Terminologies.

Keys

The Keys page lets you view and change keyboard shortcuts. The commands of Hermes 2 are in the categories File, Edit, Text Editing, Window and Healthcare LOM Help.

Preferences are stored in the workspace, ~/.hermes2/workspace. Use Restore Defaults on a page to return to the defaults.
