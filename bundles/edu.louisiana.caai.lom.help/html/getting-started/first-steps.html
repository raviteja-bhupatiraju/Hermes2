First Steps

This walk-through creates a continuing-education record in Healthcare LOM, fills in the most important fields, checks it and saves it. It takes about ten minutes. Documents of the other MedBiquitous standards are created and edited the same way; see Documents of the other standards at the end.

1. Tell Hermes 2 who you are (optional)

  1. Choose Window > Preferences… and select Healthcare LOM.
  2. Under Author, enter your full name, organization and email. The vCard preview shows what will be recorded.
  3. Keep Add me as author and meta-metadata creator of new documents selected and click Apply and Close.

New documents now list you in Life Cycle (author) and Meta-Metadata (creator).

2. Create a project (recommended)

A project keeps related records together and validates them in the background.

  1. Choose File > New > Project… (or New project… on the start page).
  2. Enter a name, check the location (by default Documents\Hermes Projects), choose whether new documents are XML or JSON, and click Create.

The project appears in the Projects view. See Creating and Opening Projects.

3. Create a record from a template

  1. Select the project in the Projects view and click New Document… on the view's tool bar. (Ctrl+N opens the same wizard, but then check that the file is created in the project folder.)
  2. Keep Healthcare LOM 1.0 (learning object metadata) as the Standard at the top of the wizard, and under Start from, choose CME course.
  3. Enter the Title, check the Language (for example en-US) and the file name, and click Finish.

The document opens in the editor. Every template is a complete, schema-valid record with placeholders such as “Author Name” for you to replace.

4. Fill in the record

Use the navigation sidebar on the left of the editor to move between pages:

  1. General: replace the description and add keywords. Click Look Up… next to the keyword list to add MeSH-coded keywords from the offline index.
  2. Life Cycle: select a contributor and replace the placeholder entity; use Add vCard… to compose a person or organization.
  3. Healthcare Education: complete the credit entry (accrediting body, credit type, number of credits), the target audience and the disclosures.

5. Check and repair

  1. Open the Overview page. The Validation card counts errors, warnings and infos; Completeness shows which recommended elements are still missing.
  2. In the Validation view at the bottom, a light bulb marks problems with a quick fix. Select one and press Ctrl+1, or click Fix All on the view's tool bar to repair every automatically fixable problem in one undoable step.
  3. Double-click any problem to jump to the field concerned.

6. Save

Press Ctrl+S. The document is written as XML (or JSON, for a .json file), and the project's documents are validated again in the background; their problems appear in the Problems view.

To experiment without risk, open a sample from the start page (Open sample…): the Healthcare LOM samples are listed first, followed by a submenu of samples for each other standard. Samples are copied to Documents\MedBiquitous Samples before they open.

Documents of the other standards

To create a Curriculum Inventory, a competency framework, an activity report or a document of any other MedBiquitous standard, choose it as the Standard in the new-document wizard (or click its card under Start from a template on the start page). The document starts from the standard's template, which is already valid, and is saved as XML. Its editor has an Overview page, form pages that follow the structure of the standard, the Document Tree and the XML page; it is validated against the standard's schemas and rules like a Healthcare LOM record. Steps 1 and 4 of this walk-through (author, MeSH keywords, credits) and the quick fixes and completeness of step 5 apply to Healthcare LOM only.
