Healthcare Professional Profile

The MedBiquitous Healthcare Professional Profile (ANSI/MEDBIQ PP.10.1-2008) is a standard XML format for profile data on healthcare professionals: identifiers, name, addresses, education, postgraduate training, specialty certification, licensure, disciplinary actions, academic appointments, occupation, personal information and memberships in professional societies. Licensing and certifying boards, professional societies, medical schools and credentialing services use it to exchange and compile credentials data, for example to verify a clinician's licenses and board certifications or to keep membership records up to date.

A profile uses two other MedBiquitous schemas: Name 1.0 for the person's name and Address 1.0 for postal addresses and countries. The Activity Report describes the person a report is about with the same Member element.

Structure of a document

The root element Members holds one or more Member elements, each the profile of “a single user or healthcare professional within a community”. A member has, in this order:

  • UniqueIDs, each with the domain that issued it (“Use of the domain attribute is required”), such as an AAMC id, a National Provider Identifier (NPI), a state license number for nurses or the id of your own organization.
  • The Name (required by the specification): formatted name, title, given and family names, generation identifier, degrees, aliases and former names (prefix n).
  • Addresses (prefix a): organizations, street address lines, city, state or province, postal code, region, district and the required Country (name and ISO 3166 code), with a category (Residential, Business, Undeliverable).
  • EducationInfo: degrees and certificates with the institution, distinction, status and the start, end and graduation dates; TrainingInfo: residencies and fellowships with the program (name, type, ID, accreditation, specialty, institution), status and dates.
  • CertificationInfo: the certification by a specialty board (organization, board, status) with its certificates (name, certificate ID, general certificate or subcertificate) and their issuances (certification ID, duration, initial, recertification or maintenance of certification, issue and expiration dates, status).
  • LicenseInfo: the licenses (licensure entity, number, initial licensure date, status, expiration date); DisciplinaryInfo: board orders with the disciplinary entity and either a board notice or a board action (description, span or term, appeal).
  • AcademicAppointmentInfo: faculty appointments (title, rank, tenure, track, effort…), chair appointments, division or section appointments and administrative appointments.
  • OccupationInfo: occupation, institution, title, privileges, practice, dates, specialties and status.
  • PersonalInfo: physical status, birth and deceased dates, languages, citizenship, country of residence, gender, race, ethnicity, telephone numbers, email addresses, web pages, tax number, family members and the preferred method of contact.
  • MembershipInfo: memberships in societies (organization, country, member number, level, role, status, dates) and in their components (chapters, special interest groups, committees).
  • ModifiedDate, the date the record was last updated, and XtensibleInfo for elements of other namespaces defined by the implementing organization.

Most parts carry a restrictions attribute: Unrestricted data “may be shared or published”, Restricted data “may be shared in some instances, but not published”, Confidential data “may not be shared or published”. Dates of education, training and certification have an accuracy (DayMonthYear, MonthYear or Year).

Rules

Besides the XML schemas, Hermes checks what the Healthcare Professional Profile, Name and Address specifications and the Implementation Guidelines state, and what follows from the meaning of the data. Problems are reported on the member, entry or element concerned; statuses are compared with the member's ModifiedDate (else today).

CheckSeverity
Each Member has a Name (“Required” in the specification).Error
A name has a FamilyName or a FormattedName (“If an individual has one name only, use FamilyName”).Warning
Each member has a UniqueID (Implementation Guidelines 3.1, identity matching 3.5), with a non-empty domain; a UniqueID is not repeated in a member (warning) and does not identify two members (error).Error / Warning
A National Provider Identifier (domain NPI) has 10 digits and a valid check digit.Warning
Date ranges are in order: start before end, graduation and completion dates of education and training, issue before expiration of certificates, initial licensure before license expiration, appointments, occupations, memberships and component groups, the span and term of board actions, notice of appeal before judgement. Samples and templates

A new document has one member with a unique ID of your organization (a generated UUID: replace the domain and the id), the name you enter as the formatted name and today's date as the modified date. The samples are:

  • physician-cardiologist.xml: a fictional academic cardiologist with bachelor's, MD and PhD degrees, residency and fellowship, board certification with maintenance of certification, two state licenses, faculty and administrative appointments, occupation with privileges, personal information and society memberships.
  • nurse-practitioner.xml: a fictional family nurse practitioner identified by a state board of nursing license number, with a certificate program in progress, a fellowship, certification and recertification, RN and APRN licenses, an adjunct faculty appointment, current and former occupations and a membership.
  • medbiq-toolkit-sample.xml: the sample of the MedBiquitous implementation toolkit, unchanged (© MedBiquitous Consortium).

The model check reports the training status “Completed Specialty Training” of the toolkit sample as not matching the pattern year \d, although the XML Schema accepts it: TrainingStatus is a union of the program years and a list of statuses, and only the program years are currently recognized by the model check.
