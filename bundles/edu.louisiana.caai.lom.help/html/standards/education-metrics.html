Medical Education Metrics (MEMS)

The MedBiquitous Medical Education Metrics standard (MEMS, ANSI/MEDBIQ ME.10.1-2009) is a common format for the outcomes of health professions education, in particular continuing education activities: how many clinicians were targeted, registered, completed the activity and received credit, how participants evaluated it and how they did on knowledge assessments. Providers, accrediting bodies, supporters and government agencies exchange and compile such reports to gauge the reach and effectiveness of activities and programs. The MEMS Survey Items define standardized survey questions with identified responses, so that evaluation data from different providers can be aggregated.

A metrics report describes its activity with an embedded Healthcare LOM record (title, identifier, credits offered, commercial support) and reports credits awarded with the credit type of the Activity Report standard.

Structure of a metrics report

The root element MedicalEducationMetrics contains evaluation data for one educational activity:

  • ReportDescription: the reporting period (ReportingStartDate and ReportingEndDate, its first and last day) and optionally a RespondentCategory, when the data cover only some participants (for example one profession).
  • ActivityDescription: the activity's Healthcare LOM record (lom; to uniquely identify the activity, use a LOM identifier such as its URL), the CommercialSupportAmount received from each source, AdvertisingAndExhibitIncome, OtherIncome and Expenses (integers with an ISO 4217 currency), and whether the activity is designed to change competence, performance or patient outcomes and whether such changes were measured.
  • ParticipantActivityEvaluation (optional): SurveyItemData, each a SurveyQuestion with its ResponseData (YesNoData, ScaleData of 3 to 10 points, MultipleChoiceSelectOneData or MultipleChoiceSelectManyData with the number of respondents per response, or FreeTextData with analysis categories), and ComparisonData: related items asked at different times (Timing Before, During or After, with a duration such as P30D). source and id attributes tie items and responses to a standardized instrument such as the MEMS Survey Items.
  • KnowledgeAssessment (optional): MultipleChoiceAssessmentItemData (a question, its responses marked correct with the number of learners who chose them, the timing and optional LOM classifications) and AssessmentScoreData (an assessment's name, ScoreMethod, one ScoreValue per learner, timing and description).
  • ParticipationMetrics: TargetedAudience, RegisteredParticipants, NumberOfParticipantsReceivingCredit, CreditsAwarded (per type of credit, the total number of credits), web statistics (NumberOfDistinctHostsOrVisitors, NumberOfSuccessfulPageRequests), NumberOfParticipantsCompletingActivity, counts by category, profession and specialty, and Comments.
  • XtensibleInfo (optional): elements of other namespaces defined by the implementing organization.

The conventional prefixes are lom and hx (Healthcare LOM); the MEMS elements use the default namespace.

Structure of a survey items document

The root element SurveyItems contains SurveyItems and Comparisons. A survey item has a SurveyQuestion and Responses: YesNo (a Yes and a No response), a Scale (a type such as likert, a number of points from 3 to 10 and the point elements One to Ten with a label), MultipleChoiceSelectOne or MultipleChoiceSelectMany (ResponseItems with their text) or FreeText. Items and responses have a source (vocabulary) and an id. A Comparison groups SurveyItemForComparisons, each with an IntendedTiming (Before, During, After and an XML Schema duration: After with P3M means three months after the activity).

The Survey Items specification publishes items with source medbiq.org: item 10 ("The course achieved its educational objectives.", a 5-point Likert scale with responses 20 to 60), the comparison items 70 and 150 (fair and balanced content, expected before and perceived after the activity, 7-point Low/High scales with responses 80 to 140 and 150 to 210), and the responses 220 and 230 (yes/no), 240 to 270 (practice setting) and 280 to 310 (barriers to treatment). Hermes resolves references to them in both document types.

Samples and templates

A new metrics report starts with a reporting period of today, an activity with a Healthcare LOM title and identifier and empty participant counts; a new survey items document starts with one 5-point Likert item. The samples are:

  • online-cme-course-report.xml: a fictional online CME/CNE course on opioid use disorder, with participation by profession and specialty, credits awarded, MedBiquitous survey items, comparison data and a pre/post knowledge assessment.
  • live-workshop-report.xml: a fictional one-day workshop with commercial support, a 30-day follow-up comparison and the pre-test and follow-up scores of each learner.
  • medbiq-survey-items.xml: the survey items of the Survey Items specification (source medbiq.org).
  • provider-evaluation-items.xml: a fictional provider's evaluation items, including "The activity was free of commercial bias." and a confidence comparison with a three-month follow-up.
