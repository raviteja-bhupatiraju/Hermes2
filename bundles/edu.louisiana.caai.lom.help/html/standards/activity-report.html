Activity Report

The MedBiquitous Activity Report records the learning and certification activities of physicians and other healthcare professionals: who registered for, completed or let expire which activity, when, with which results and which credits. Continuing education providers, specialty societies, certifying and licensing boards and portfolio systems exchange activity reports, for example as digital CE certificates and transcripts or to track Maintenance of Certification (MoC) activities.

Activity reports reuse two other MedBiquitous standards: the person is described with the Healthcare Professional Profile (Member), and an activity can be described with an embedded Healthcare LOM record (lom), whose healthcare extension also lists the credits the activity offers.

Structure of a document

The root element ActivityReports holds the date and time the reports were created (DateTimeCreated; in 2.0 also ReportingStartDate and ReportingEndDate, the first and last day of the reporting period, and an arid) and one or more ActivityReport elements. Each activity report contains:

  • ReportingOrganization: the organization that is the source of the report.
  • Member: the single person the report is about (Professional Profile: UniqueID with a domain, Name, licenses…). In 2.0 a report has a Member and/or Participants: anonymous demographics of the learners of an activity (local identifier, state of primary practice, DEA registration, profession, practice area, surgical procedures, time in practice).
  • One or more Activity elements: the provider organization, the activity name (for continuing education, the title of the course) and its Modules. An activity without parts has one module named like the activity; a broad activity (such as a quality improvement program) has a module per topic.
  • For each module: optional Metadata (an embedded lom record or the location of one), the Status (Registered, Completed or Expired), the registration, start, due, end, completion and expiration dates, the TimeEngaged (an ISO 8601 duration such as PT1H30M), the Results and one CreditCertificate per type of credit received: the CreditReceived (accrediting body, activity certification such as AMA PRA Category 1 Credit, credit type and unit, number of credits, optional credit focus categories) and a globally unique CreditID (ccid:providerdomain:localidentifier).
  • Optionally PointOfCareLearningData: the clinical question, the search parameters, the resources used and the application to practice, as required for Internet point-of-care learning credit.

Elements of other namespaces can extend ActivityReports, ActivityReport, Activity and Module in 2.0 (the preferred method); XtensibleInfo remains for backward compatibility. The conventional prefixes are m (member), n (name), a (address), lom and hx (Healthcare LOM).

Reports are exchanged as documents or through the Activity Report web services, whose create, read, update and delete operations take an ActivityReports document.

Pages list the activity reports, then the activities of the selected report, then its modules: select an item to edit its details. The values of unique IDs and regulation URIs are edited in a list, their domain and label attributes in the list below it. Everything else (extensions, the rest of the Professional Profile and of the Healthcare LOM record) is edited in the tree or on the XML page.

Rules

Besides the XML schemas, Hermes checks what the specification (Activity Report Specifications 1.54) and the Activity Report Implementation Guidelines 1.0 state, and what follows from the meaning of the data:

CheckSeverity
The reporting period runs forward: ReportingStartDate is not after ReportingEndDate (2.0).Error
Completion and expiration dates of modules fall within the reporting period (2.0); the reports are not created before the period starts nor in the future.Warning
Dates of a module are in order: start before end, registration and start before completion, registration before expiration (errors); due date not before registration (warning).Error / Warning
A Completed module has a CompletedDateTime (guidelines); a module with a completion date is Completed; an Expired module has an ExpiredDateTime and a Registered one a registration or start date (info).Warning / Info
A Completed module whose metadata offers credits reports the credits received; credit certificates belong to Completed modules, and not to modules whose metadata says no credits are available.Warning
The credits received are given, positive and not above the credits the module's Healthcare LOM metadata offers for that certification (or credit type and unit); a credit focus is not larger than its certificate; one certificate per type of credit.Warning
CreditID uses ccid:providerdomain:localidentifier (warning) and is unique (error); arid uses arid:domain:localidentifier (warning) and is unique (error). Error / Warning
A Member is identified: by a UniqueID or at least a name (error); with a UniqueID and its domain, as the guidelines recommend (warning).Error / Warning
An activity report has at most one Member and one Participants element (2.0).Error
No duplicates: the same person in two reports, the same participant twice, the same activity twice in a report or the same module twice in an activity.Warning
LaunchAction is one of the recommended values Noaction, Activity, CE, Registry (2.0). Info

Samples and templates

New documents of either version start with one report on one member registered for one activity whose module is described by a Healthcare LOM title. The samples (fictional people and organizations) are:

  • cme-course-completions-v1.xml, cme-course-completions-v2.xml: a live CME course completed by three physicians, with the AMA PRA Category 1 credits (and MOC points) they received.
  • registration-in-progress-v1.xml: a self-assessment activity with a completed, a registered and an expired module; registration-in-progress-v2.xml: a nurse registered for an online course, with launch details.
  • rems-participants-v2.xml: learner demographics of an Opioid Analgesic REMS compliant activity.
