Offline Terminologies

Complete terminologies ship with Hermes 2 and are searched without a network connection: in keyword lists (Look Up…), with Add MeSH Keywords, for classification taxon paths, in content assist on the XML and JSON pages, and by validation. These lookups are used in Healthcare LOM records.

Window > Preferences… > Healthcare LOM > Terminologies lists the terminologies with their version, source and license notice; each lookup shows the notice in its details.

Source names

Hermes 2 writes MeSH and ICD-10-CM as keyword source. For taxon paths it writes the terminology's name, for example “ACGME Core Competencies”. When reading sources, common aliases are recognized as well, for example Medical Subject Headings, MSH, ICD-10, ICD10CM, ACGME Competencies, Bloom's Taxonomy or Curriculum Inventory.

MeSH online fallback

The NLM MeSH service is used only if you turn on Look up unknown descriptor IDs online (Preferences > Healthcare LOM > MeSH), and then only for descriptor IDs newer than the offline index. The default service is the NLM MeSH RDF API, https://id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/.

Terminologies that are not included

SNOMED CT, UMLS and LOINC require a license of each user and are not included. You can still enter them as keyword or taxon sources by hand (for example hx:source="SNOMED CT" with the concept ID in hx:id); their codes are not checked.

Licenses

  • MeSH: courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine, used under the NLM terms and conditions; the index contains a subset of the descriptor data (names, entry terms, scope notes, tree numbers, see-also references) and is not an official NLM product.
  • ICD-10-CM: published by NCHS and in the public domain in the United States (ICD-10 copyright World Health Organization).
  • AAMC Curriculum Inventory vocabulary: Copyright 2016 Association of American Medical Colleges, used under the MedBiquitous Standards Public License; converted to a searchable index, wording unchanged.
  • ACGME and Bloom: category names and codes; descriptions summarized.

For developers: the indexes are terminology/*.tsv.gz in the bundle edu.louisiana.caai.lom.model. They are generated from the published sources by terminology-src/build_terminologies.py; its README lists the downloads and how to regenerate them.
